India through the Seasons

India's climate changes a lot between the northern mountains and the southern tropics. Use this planner to choose when and where to go.

October to March

Winter

Best for Rajasthan's deserts, the Taj Mahal and Varanasi's ghats.

April to June

Summer

Head for cooler places: Himachal Pradesh, Ladakh and Munnar's tea estates.

July to September

Monsoon

Green landscapes in Kerala and the Western Ghats.
