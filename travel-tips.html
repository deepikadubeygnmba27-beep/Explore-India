Getting Around

India's rail network is scenic and busy, and domestic flights link the major hubs in a few hours. For short trips, take local rickshaws or hire a private driver.

Book long-distance train travel at least two weeks ahead to get the most comfortable berths.

Heritage Havens

Stay in places with a story, from historic havelis in the Thar Desert to calm retreats in Kerala. Old architecture meets modern handcrafted luxury.

Before You Go

Taste of India

Try regional thalis, street snacks from busy stalls and fresh chai. Ask for the spice level that suits you.

Travel Securely

Keep copies of your passport and visa, use licensed taxis or app-based rides, drink bottled or filtered water, and buy travel insurance before you leave.

Essential Gear

Pack light cotton layers, a scarf for sun and temples, comfortable walking shoes, a power bank and a universal adapter.

Cultural Respect

Dress modestly at temples, remove your shoes where asked, ask before photographing people, and use your right hand to give and receive.
